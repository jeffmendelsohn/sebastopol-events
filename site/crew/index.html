Opens a short form on GitHub (free account). It goes on the calendar once reviewed.

Paste the link to wherever your group keeps its events. It is read every morning.

Luma, Google Calendar, Meetup, Eventbrite, Mobilize, Tockify, .ics or webcal, RSS, Squarespace and WordPress events pages.

Luma (easiest)

  1. Sign up free at luma.com.
  2. Create a calendar for your group and add events.
  3. Paste its link under .

Google Calendar

  1. Make a calendar for your group's public events.
  2. In its settings, tick "Make available to public".
  3. Copy "Public address in iCal format" and paste it under .
